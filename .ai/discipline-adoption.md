# Discipline adoption inventory

The local discipline-adoption inventory required by
`SPECIFICATION/non-functional-requirements.md` §"Discipline adoption
inventory". It classifies every seed-listed livespec fleet discipline for
this standalone TypeScript/Svelte/Bun/Vercel repository. Shape and
citations are verified by the discipline-inventory gate in `bun run check`
(`scripts/check-discipline-inventory.ts`): dispositions and enforcement
classes must come from the allowed sets, gate-/process-enforced rows must
cite artifacts that actually exist, documented-only rows must not claim
enforcement, and deferred/rejected rows must state a reason and a revisit
condition.

Dispositions: adopted, locally adapted, deferred, rejected.
Enforcement classes: gate-enforced, process-enforced, documented-only, none.
